Slidora – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Developer: Atlass Apps (published on Google Play by Mohamed El Maataouy) · Contact: hamid.dev2030@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Slidora mobile game ("Slidora", "the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. Slidora is developed by Atlass Apps and published on Google Play under the developer name Mohamed El Maataouy (together "we", "us"). We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts or precise location.

2. Advertising and your consent

Slidora is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Shop → Privacy settings in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Learn how Google uses information from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Third-party services

These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore): policies.google.com/privacy · firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play (purchases, in-app review): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudinary: cloudinary.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Notifications and permissions

If you allow notifications, Slidora schedules reminders (for example, your daily puzzle) directly on your device. They are created locally and do not send data to us. The app picks photos only through the system photo picker when you choose to; it does not read your gallery otherwise.

5. How long we keep data

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Challenge data in Cloud Firestore is kept so that shared codes keep working; you can ask us to delete a challenge at any time (see section 7). Analytics and advertising data are kept by Google according to its retention settings.

6. Security

All data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our Firebase project is restricted.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect your name or e-mail address, we can only find a challenge if you give us its code. To make a request, write to hamid.dev2030@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

To delete all game data stored on your device, uninstall Slidora or clear its storage in your device settings.

8. Children

Slidora is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
